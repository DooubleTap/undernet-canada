Getting in

Three steps, about two minutes

IRC has no sign-up. You pick a client, point it at a server, and join the room. That's the whole thing.

1

Pick a client

Any IRC client works. In a hurry? Skip this and use the webchat below — nothing to install.

2

Connect to Undernet

Point it at the Undernet round-robin. Canadians can use the regional alias instead.

/server irc.undernet.org 6667
/server ca.undernet.org 6667
3

Join the channel

Pick any nickname that isn't taken, then join. Say hello — someone will answer.

/join #Canada
/nick your_nick
About encryption: Undernet does not offer TLS — the usual secure port 6697 is closed. Your connection to the network is in the clear, so treat anything you type as public. If you want an encrypted hop, use the browser webchat or a bouncer such as ZNC.

Channel bot

OhCanada

OhCanada keeps the room running: it hands out voice to people who join the conversation, holds the topic in place, helps the ops with bans and modes, and answers questions about itself.

Command What it does
!helpEverything it can do — answered by notice, never in the channel
!help <command>Usage, description and an example for one command
/msg OhCanada registerRegister yourself with the bot — it sends you a code to type back
!verifyYour access level, flags and registered hosts
!info <text>Set the infoline people see in your whois
!ops <reason>Quietly page the channel staff when something is wrong
!bansWhat's on the banlist right now

Every reply arrives as a private notice, so nobody has to watch the channel fill up with bot output.

Registering? Hide your host first. OhCanada identifies you by your hostname. Auth with X and set usermode +x before you register, so it stores your account.users.undernet.org host instead of your ISP one — nobody else can wear that host, and it does not change when your IP does.

House rules

Six things, that's it

Nothing here should surprise anyone. The ops enforce it with a light hand, and !ops reaches them if you need help.

01
Be respectful Treat everyone with courtesy, whatever their background or opinions.
02
No spam or flooding No repeated messages, pastes of a hundred lines, or advertising.
03
Keep it roughly on topic Tangents are fine — long private conversations belong in a query.
04
No harassment Trolling, targeting people, and abuse get you removed. No warnings owed.
05
Both languages are welcome English, français, or switching mid-sentence. Nobody has to translate for anybody.
06
Nothing illegal No warez, no illegal material, no arranging any of it here.
Questions

Things people ask

Do I need to register a nickname?
No — Undernet has no NickServ and nicknames aren't owned by anyone. Pick one and use it. What does get registered on Undernet is channels, through CService and the X bot.
Why did the bot give me a + next to my name?
That's voice (+v). OhCanada gives it to people who join the conversation, and takes it back after a long stretch of silence. It's not a rank — it just marks who's actually around.
How do I register with the bot?
Auth with X and set /mode yournick +x first, then send /msg OhCanada register. The bot replies with a one-time code and you type it back — that is all. It stores a handle and your hostname, and grants no access flags; a channel op hands those out. The full walkthrough is in the bot guide.
Is the connection encrypted?
Not to Undernet itself — the network doesn't run TLS, so port 6697 won't answer. The browser webchat encrypts the hop between you and the webchat provider, and a bouncer like ZNC does the same. Either way, treat the channel as a public room.
How do I stay connected when my computer is off?
Run a bouncer — ZNC on a cheap VPS is the usual answer — or use IRCCloud, which does the same thing as a service. Both keep your nick in the channel and replay what you missed.
Something's wrong in the channel. Who do I tell?
Type !ops followed by what's happening. It pages the channel staff privately — the channel doesn't see it, and neither does whoever caused the problem.
I've never used IRC. Is it dead?
It's older than the web and still running. No accounts, no feed, no engagement metrics — a channel is just people in a room, and it stays open whether or not anyone is talking. Join, idle, come back tomorrow.
